Training overview

Creative and innovative behavioral competency enhances an employee's ability to adapt to modern and new changes and apply them to the existing situation. The training is designed to strengthen it for four purposes.

Identifying new ways of working
Predicting the future focuses
Understanding and implementing appropriate response
Promoting and implementing study findings that improve service delivery and productivity

Training contents

  1. Introduction to creativity and innovation
  2. Creativity and innovation process steps
  3. Sources and importance of creativity and innovation
  4. Team-based creativity and innovation
  5. Creativity and innovation-based perspectives
  6. Entrepreneurial mindset
  7. Understanding and implementing new practices and changes

Training objectives

The training aims to enhance your ability to generate new creative and innovation-oriented operational processes and to implement new practices and changes. At the end you will be able to:

  • Generate new creative and innovation-oriented operation processes
  • Identify the importance of creativity and innovation
  • Analyze the importance of team-based creativity and innovation
  • Implement organizational operational processes and systems
  • Encourage and accept creativity and innovation-based perspectives
  • Generate positive entrepreneurial mindsets for creativity and innovation
  • Implement new practices and changes in creativity and innovation operational processes

1. Introduction and basic theories

By the end of this unit you will be able to describe the basic theories of creativity and innovation, identify the nature of creative and innovative processes, and generate new operational processes.

Creativity
The ability to generate new and original ideas. It is marked by originality, imagination, and divergent thinking.
Innovation
Implementing creative ideas in a way that adds value. It is marked by practical application, value creation, and change, and can be incremental or disruptive.
The difference
Creativity is thinking of new things and focuses on new ideas. Innovation is doing new things and focuses on implementation, and it carries time and resource risk.
Basic theories
Shrinking Earth, Systems, Honing, Everyday Imaginative Thought, Evolution, Investment, and Social Personality theories. Each comes with practical ways to apply it in a team.
Brainstorming questions. What is creativity? What is innovation? Compare creativity and innovation.
2. Creativity and innovation process steps

By the end of this unit you will be able to identify the steps of creative and innovative processes, see their effect on operational effectiveness, and develop problem-solving processes.

Wallas model
Preparation, incubation, illumination, and verification.
Barron model
Conception, gestation, parturition, and bringing up the baby.
Creative problem solving
Objective finding, fact finding, problem finding, idea finding, solution finding, and acceptance finding.
Directed creativity cycle
Preparation, imagination, development, and action.
Stage-gate innovation model
Idea generation, idea screening, concept development and testing, business analysis, product development, market testing, commercialization, and post-launch review and continuous improvement.
Reflective activities 2.1 and 2.2. Identify the steps, and explain their impact on the operational effectiveness of organizations.

See the worked example: online student services (Amharic)

3. Sources and importance of creativity and innovation

By the end of this unit you will be able to identify the sources of creativity and innovation, analyze their importance, and identify institutional arrangements and practices that support creative projects.

Sources of creativity
Personal experiences, cultural influences, social interactions, environmental factors, technology and tools, education and learning, growth mindset and divergent thinking, economic and political contexts, global trends, and accidental discoveries.
Sources of innovation
A recurring problem in the work unit, exposure to new ideas and trends, listening to customers, talking with creative thinkers, and researching how new technologies are used.
Importance
Competitive advantage, efficiency, better customer experience, economic growth, sustainability, employee engagement, and better problem solving.
Impacts
On individuals (personal growth and career), organizations (competitiveness and sustainability), and communities (economic development and social cohesion).
Activities 3.1 and 3.2, group discussions. Present your reflections on sources, importance, impacts, and the institutional arrangements that support creative and innovative projects.
4. Team-based creativity and innovation

By the end of this unit you will be able to identify the nature of team-based creativity and innovation, analyze its importance, and implement it.

Nature
A collaborative process in which a group generates novel and useful ideas. Key aspects include diversity of thought, shared goals, open communication, and continuous learning.
Importance
Diverse perspectives, higher motivation and engagement, stronger problem solving, better decisions, knowledge sharing, and stronger team cohesion.
Building effective teams
Look at the team task, team composition, organizational context, and team processes. Reward genuine attempts at innovation, set team norms, encourage reflection, and manage conflict constructively.
Activity 4.1, group discussion. Explain the nature and importance of team-based creativity, how to foster it, its challenges compared with individual work, and ways to reduce them.
5. Creativity and innovation-based perspectives

By the end of this unit you will be able to identify opportunities for creativity and innovation, develop a continuous curiosity to realize them, and encourage creativity and innovation-based perspectives.

Accepting others' ideas
Practice active listening, try to understand the emotions and motives behind a view, ask open-ended questions, stay open-minded, and acknowledge contributions even when you disagree.
Supporting efforts
Encourage open communication without fear of criticism, collaborate across backgrounds, set aside resources and time for creative projects, and join programs where ideas can be pitched and mentored.
Promoting creativity
Build a supportive culture, provide tools and learning, foster collaboration, encourage experimentation, set clear innovation goals, reward creativity, and keep learning.
Activity 5.1, group discussion. Share how you support creative efforts in your organization, the challenges of accepting others' ideas, and strategies that can support creativity and innovation.
6. Entrepreneurial mindset

By the end of this unit you will be able to identify the benefits and challenges for creativity and innovation, develop a continuous curiosity, and generate a positive entrepreneurial mindset.

Nature
Entrepreneurs hold a clear vision with measurable goals, think innovatively, spot opportunities where others see obstacles, pivot when conditions change, and treat failure as a stepping stone.
Growth mindset
The belief that abilities develop through effort. It embraces challenges, persists after setbacks, values effort, seeks feedback, and draws inspiration from others' success.
Fixed mindset
The belief that abilities are static. It avoids difficult tasks, gives up when obstacles appear, and treats criticism as a personal attack.
Enhancing it
Join entrepreneurship and innovation training, take on problem-solving challenges, work with diverse people, manage emotions under stress, explore fields outside your own, assess risks through scenarios, and adopt a growth mindset.
Reflective activities 6.1 and 6.2. Describe the benefits of an entrepreneurial mindset, contrast growth and fixed mindsets, and explain how the mindset shapes creativity and innovation.
7. Understanding and implementing new practices and changes

By the end of this unit you will be able to identify operational gaps, analyze new practices and changes, and implement them in creativity and innovation operational processes.

Understanding the need
Evaluate existing methods, investigate best practices, use performance metrics, weigh benefits and risks, and engage the people who will be affected.
Implementing
Develop a clear plan, communicate well, encourage participation and feedback, monitor progress, celebrate successes, and review after implementation.
Models
The comprehensive approach model for innovation enhancement, and the componential model of creativity and innovation in organizations.
Framework process
Stakeholder engagement, benchmarking, framework development, competency assessment, training, integration with HR systems, and performance metrics.
Resistance
A natural response. Understand concerns, give a clear rationale, involve resistors in solutions, and stay patient and supportive.
Activities 7.1 and 7.2, group discussions. Identify the key implementation processes, ways to address resistance, metrics and feedback methods, and how to select an appropriate model.

    • ወይስ የተማሪዎች ሞባይል አፕሊኬሽን ማልማት? የሚሉ ሐሳቦች ይፈልቃሉ።

  2. Idea screening ሐሳብን ማጣራትና መምረጥ

    ተግባር፦ ከተነሱት ሐሳቦች ውስጥ የተማሪውን ሚስጥራዊ መረጃ የሚጠብቀውን፣ ለተቋሙ ወጪ ቆጣቢ የሆነውን እና ለረጅም ጊዜ የሚያገለግለውን እንመርጣለን።

    ውሳኔ፦ ጎግል ፎርም የደህንነት እና የዳታ ውህደት (Integration) ውስንነት ስላለው፤ እንዲሁም ሞባይል አፕ ውድ ስለሚሆን ➔ “በተቋሙ ዌብሳይት ውስጥ የተካተተ ደህንነቱ የተጠበቀ የኦንላይን ፎርም (Online Web Portal)” እንዲሰራ ይመረጣል።

  3. Concept development and testing ጽንሰ-ሐሳብን ማጎልበት እና መሞከር

    ተግባር፦ ፎርሙ ምን ምን አገልግሎቶችን እንደሚያካትት በንድፍ (Prototype/Wireframe) ደረጃ ማዘጋጀት እና የተጠቃሚዎችን አስተያየት መውሰድ።

    • በወረቀት ወይም በኮምፒውተር ዲዛይን (Mockup) ተማሪዎች መታወቂያቸውን አስገብተው፣ የሚፈልጉትን አገልግሎት (ለምሳሌ፡ የክፍል ቅያሬ፣ የምስክር ወረቀት፣ የዶርም ቅሬታ...) የሚመርጡበትን ገጽ ማዘጋጀት።
    • ይህን ንድፍ ለተወሰኑ ተማሪዎችና ሰራተኞች አሳይቶ አስተያየት መቀበል (“ለመጠቀም ግልጽ ነው? ይቀላል?”)።

  4. Business analysis የፋይዳ እና የወጪ ትንተና

    ተግባር፦ ሲስተሙን መገንባት ምን ያህል ወጪ እንደሚያስወጣ እና ምን ያህል ጥቅም እንደሚያስገኝ መገምገም።

    • ወጪ፦ የሶፍትዌር ማበልጸጊያ ወጪ፣ የሰርቨር (Server) እና የጥገና ወጪ።
    • ጥቅም፦ በወረቀት፣ በማተሚያ እና በሰራተኛ ጊዜ ይባክን የነበረውን ገንዘብ ማዳን፤ የተማሪዎችን እርካታ በከፍተኛ ደረጃ መጨመር፤ መረጃዎች ሳይጠፉ በአንድ ቋት (Database) እንዲቀመጡ ማድረግ።

  5. Product development ምርቱን/ሲስተሙን ማልማት

    ተግባር፦ የአይቲ (IT) ባለሙያዎች ትክክለኛውን የሚሰራ የኦንላይን ፎርም ሲስተም መገንባት ይጀምራሉ።

    • ተማሪው ፎርሙን ሞልቶ የሚያስገባበት (Frontend)፣
    • ፎርሙ በቀጥታ ለሚመለከተው የተማሪዎች አገልግሎት ሰራተኛ የሚደርስበት እና ዳታው የሚቀመጥበት (Backend & Database) ማዘጋጀት።

  6. Quality approval የጥራት ማረጋገጫና ይሁንታ

    ተግባር፦ ሲስተሙ ከመሰራጨቱ በፊት አስተማማኝነቱ እና ጥራቱ በባለሙያዎች ይመረመራል።

    • ሲስተሙ አይበላሽም? (Bug testing)
    • የተማሪዎች ሚስጥራዊ መረጃ ይጠበቃል? (Security/Privacy test)
    • በአንድ ጊዜ ሺህ ተማሪ ቢገባ ሲስተሙ ይቆማል ወይስ ይቋቋማል? (Load testing)
    • የአይቲ ዳይሬክቶሬት እና የተማሪዎች አገልግሎት ኃላፊዎች ስርዓቱ ብቁ መሆኑን አረጋግጠው ይሁንታ (Approval) ይሰጣሉ።

  7. Market testing የሙከራ ደረጃ / Pilot Testing

    ተግባር፦ ለአጠቃላይ ተማሪ ከመልቀቃችን በፊት በአነስተኛ ደረጃ ሞክሮ ማየት።

    • ለአንድ ፋኩልቲ ወይም ለአንድ ክፍል (ለምሳሌ ለ 200 ተማሪዎች ብቻ) ለሁለት ሳምንት አገልግሎቱን በኦንላይን እንዲጠይቁ ማድረግ።
    • በሙከራው ወቅት የታዩ ክፍተቶችን (ለምሳሌ ፎርም ሲላክ ሰራተኛ ጋር አለመድረስ፣ ወይም ፎርማቱ ግራ የሚያጋባ መሆን) ማስተካከል።

  8. Commercialization ይፋ ማድረግ እና ስራ ላይ ማዋል

    ተግባር፦ ሲስተሙን ለመላው ተማሪዎች በሙሉ አቅም ክፍት ማድረግ።

    • “ከዛሬ ጀምሮ ማንኛውም የተማሪዎች አገልግሎት በኦንላይን ፎርም ብቻ ይሰጣል” የሚል ማስታወቂያ ማውጣት።
    • ተማሪዎች እንዴት መሙላት እንዳለባቸው አጭር የቪዲዮ ወይም የጽሁፍ መመሪያ ማሰራጨት።
    • የወረቀት አሰራርን ሙሉ በሙሉ ማስቆም።

  9. Post-launch review and continuous improvement ከስራ ማስጀመር በኋላ የሚደረግ ግምገማ እና ቀጣይነት ያለው ማሻሻያ

    ተግባር፦ ስርዓቱ ስራ ከጀመረ ከ 3 ወይም ከ 6 ወራት በኋላ ያለውን ሁኔታ መገምገም እና አዳዲስ ነገሮችን መጨመር።

    • ተማሪዎች ጥያቄያቸው በምን ደረጃ ላይ እንዳለ በስልካቸው ማየት እንዲችሉ የኤስኤምኤስ (SMS Notification) መጨመር።
    • ስርዓቱ አገልግሎት አሰጣጡን ምን ያህል እንዳፋጠነው ሪፖርት አዘጋጅቶ ለቀጣይ አዳዲስ የፈጠራ ስራዎች እንደ መነሻ መጠቀም (ዑደቱ እንደገና ይቀጥላል)።

የዋላስ ሞዴል (Wallas Model)

በሰው አእምሮ ውስጥ የፈጠራ ሐሳቡ እንዴት እንደተወለደ የሚያሳይ የስነ-ልቦና ሂደት ነው።

የስቴጅ-ጌት ሞዴል (Stage-Gate Model)

የተወለደውን ሐሳብ ተቋማዊ አድርጎ፣ በጀት መድቦለትና ፈትሾ ወደ ትልቅ ፕሮጀክት እንዴት እንደሚቀየር የሚያሳይ ዝርዝር የስራ ሂደት ነው።
